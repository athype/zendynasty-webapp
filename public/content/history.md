# Our history

Reddit Zen was founded back in 2014, beginning as a small, humble but committed clan. Our first feeder clan, Reddit Zenith, became the starting point for many of our earliest members.
<br>
Since then, we’ve grown into a thriving community with 5 competitive war clans, 2 GFL clans, and numerous of event clans under our banner of the “Reddit Zen Dynasty”.

# Alliances

The Reddit Zen Dynasty is part of the larger Reddit Clan System, a network of clans built on shared values of competition, community, and growth.
<br>
In the summer of 2024, we formed a key partnership with Lethal Demise, another highly competitive and respected family of clans.
